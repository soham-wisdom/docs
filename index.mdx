WisdomAI lets you ask questions about your business data in plain language and get back answers, charts, dashboards, and apps. Every answer is grounded in the context your data team defines: what your tables mean, how your metrics are calculated, and who is allowed to see what.

<PlatformDiagram />

For a closer look at each capability, see [How WisdomAI Works](/getting-started/how-wisdom-ai-works).
