## How WisdomAI is different

Generic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) tools search over documents. WisdomAI builds a structured understanding of your data through semantic modeling, curated context, and validated logic, so it is purpose-built for enterprise analytics, not just document Q&A.

| **Feature** | **WisdomAI** | **Generic RAG** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understands data relationships | ✅ | ❌ |
| Uses a semantic model of your business | ✅ | ❌ |
| Validated, testable logic | ✅ | ❌ |
| Dynamic, auto-updating dashboards and apps | ✅ | ❌ |
| Tailored for business data | ✅ | ❌ |
