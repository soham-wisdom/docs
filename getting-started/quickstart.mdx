Get started in 3 steps. Each step belongs to a role, so jump straight to yours.

<Steps>
  <Step icon="plug" title="Connect your data">
    **Admins** connect your data warehouse, such as Snowflake, BigQuery, or Databricks, and manage who can see what. [Start here](/getting-started/connect-data-sources/supported-data-sources).
  </Step>
  <Step icon="sitemap" title="Add your business context">
    **Data teams** organize tables into domains and describe what they mean, so answers match how your business talks about its data. [Start here](/setting-up-wisdom-ai/getting-started-domains-context).
  </Step>
  <Step icon="comments" title="Ask, analyze, and share">
    **Explorers** ask questions in plain language and turn answers into dashboards and Live Apps. [Start here](/explorers/getting-started-explorers).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Building on WisdomAI from your own product? See the [Developers](/integrations/developers) docs.

## Try it hands-on

For a step-by-step walkthrough, go to the [Connect and Test Tutorial](/setting-up-wisdom-ai/basic-tutorial-connect-and-test). It shows admins how to connect a data source, ask the first questions, and give feedback that improves answers.
