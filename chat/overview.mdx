WisdomAI Chat is a natural language interface for your connected business data. Ask a question in plain language, and WisdomAI Chat interprets it, translates it into SQL using the NLQ (natural language query) engine, and returns a structured response: a text explanation, a data table, a visualization, or a combination of the three. You can turn any answer into an interactive dashboard and share it with your team.

## How the WisdomAI chat works

WisdomAI turns a plain text question into an answer through five stages:

1. **Ask a question.** Type a question in natural language, such as "What was revenue last quarter?"
2. **NLQ Engine parses the question.** The engine interprets your question and translates it into SQL, using your semantic model to map business terms (like "revenue") to the underlying data.
3. **WisdomAI generates an answer.** Depending on the question, the answer appears as text, a data table, or a visualization.
4. **Build and share a dashboard.** Turn any answer into a dashboard, then share it with your team or schedule automated delivery.
5. **Feedback improves future answers.** Correct an answer, and WisdomAI uses that correction to improve accuracy on similar questions going forward.

## Recurring Agents in chat

When your question implies a recurring or scheduled need, WisdomAI displays a **Create Agent button** under the chat answer, letting you turn that one-off analysis into an automated Agent. To learn more about Agents, see [Agent Overview](/advanced-features/agents/agents).
