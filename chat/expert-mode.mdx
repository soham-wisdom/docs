---
title: "Expert Mode"
---

Expert is the reasoning mode for complex, open-ended questions, such as "Why did our revenue spike last quarter?" or "What are the main drivers of customer churn?". Instead of a single query, WisdomAI breaks the question into a multi-step investigation, runs each step, and synthesizes the findings into a cited report.

Unlike the previous Deep Analysis toggle, Expert runs inside the normal chat flow. There's no separate feature to enable, no plan to review and approve first: pick the mode, ask your question, and the investigation starts.

## Run an investigation

1. **Select Expert** from the mode selector under the chat input (next to Thinking and Lite).
2. **Ask an open-ended question.** These often start with "Why," "Investigate," or "What are the main drivers of...".
3. **Answer any clarifying questions.** If your question is ambiguous, or WisdomAI hits a data gap partway through (for example, a comparison period with no matching data), it pauses and asks, with the likely interpretations as clickable options, the same clarification UI used everywhere else in chat. Pick one, or type your own answer.
4. **Watch the investigation run.** A **Steps** panel lists the stages of the investigation and checks each one off as it completes. WisdomAI often runs several sub-investigations in parallel, each inspectable on its own, showing the context it used, the SQL it executed, and the output table it produced.
5. **Review the report.** When the investigation finishes, the findings open as a report: an executive summary, followed by sections with inline citations linking back to the specific evidence (a chart, a data table, a reconciliation) behind each claim.

## Work with the report

From the report panel you can:

- **Export as PDF** or **Word (DOCX)**
- **Share via link**, so teammates can open the report directly
- Continue the conversation with follow-up questions, such as `generate a shorter one page version of the report`

## Use case examples

Expert mode is ideal for situations where a single data point isn't enough to understand the full picture.

### Investigating a performance spike

This is a primary use case for conducting root cause analysis when a key metric changes unexpectedly.

- **Scenario**: You notice a sudden increase in user engagement for a specific feature and need to understand the cause.
- **Question**: `Investigate the key drivers for the spike in user engagement for the 'new dashboard' feature last month.`
- **Outcome**: WisdomAI generates a report identifying the primary causes, such as a recent marketing campaign, a new integration partner driving traffic, or a specific segment of users heavily adopting the feature, with charts breaking down engagement by user segment and traffic source.

### Understanding complex business trends

Use Expert mode for exploratory analysis when the factors influencing a trend are not immediately apparent.

- **Scenario**: Your quarterly report shows an increase in customer churn, but the high-level numbers don't explain why it's happening.
- **Question**: `What are the primary factors contributing to customer churn in Q2?`
- **Outcome**: WisdomAI segments churned customers by subscription plan, region, product usage, and signup date, and the report reveals that customers on a specific plan who don't adopt a key feature within their first 30 days are the most likely to churn, actionable insight for the product team.
